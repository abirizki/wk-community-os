# Product Overview

## Introduction

WK Community OS is a comprehensive, cloud-native platform designed to digitize and streamline administrative services at the local government level (Kelurahan, RW, RT). Built on the highly scalable and secure Google Cloud Platform (via Google Apps Script), it provides an integrated suite of tools for citizen data management, public service requests, and operational analytics.

## Core Modules & Features

The system is built on a modular architecture, ensuring extensibility and maintainability.

### 1. Citizen & Household Management
*   **Centralized Citizen Registry:** Securely manage comprehensive citizen data (NIK, KK, address, demographics).
*   **Household Management:** Group citizens into households (Kartu Keluarga) with a designated head of household.
*   **Demographic Analysis:** Track population statistics, including age groups, gender distribution, and resident status.

### 2. Letter Service (Layanan Surat)
*   **Digital Letter Requests:** Citizens can request various official letters online (e.g., SKTM, Domisili, Surat Pengantar).
*   **Automated Workflow:** Multi-level approval process from RT, RW, to Kelurahan.
*   **PDF Generation:** Automatic generation of official, templated PDF documents.
*   **QR Code Verification:** Each letter includes a unique QR code for instant online verification, preventing fraud.

### 3. Complaint Management (Layanan Aduan)
*   **Public Complaint Submission:** A structured channel for citizens to submit complaints regarding public services (e.g., infrastructure, social issues).
*   **Categorization & Prioritization:** Complaints are categorized and prioritized for efficient handling.
*   **Assignment & Resolution Workflow:** Complaints are routed to the appropriate officer for resolution.
*   **Public Tracking:** Citizens can track the status of their complaints using a public tracking number.

### 4. Workflow Engine
*   A universal, state-driven engine that powers all approval processes, ensuring consistency and auditability.

### 5. Notification Center
*   **Multi-Channel Alerts:** Automatic notifications are sent to both citizens and officials via an in-app inbox, push notifications, and email.
*   **Event-Driven:** Notifications are triggered by key events, such as a new letter request or a change in complaint status.
*   **Broadcast Functionality:** Administrators can broadcast important announcements to specific groups (e.g., all citizens in RT 001).

### 6. Analytics & Dashboards
*   **Role-Based Dashboards:** Dedicated dashboards for RT, RW, and Kelurahan staff, providing at-a-glance operational insights.
*   **Pre-aggregated Data:** Dashboards are highly performant, reading from pre-calculated analytics data.
*   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Track metrics like average letter processing time, complaint resolution rate, and population growth.
*   **Reporting:** Generate detailed reports for administrative and executive needs.

### 7. Deployment & Maintenance Toolkit
*   **Environment Management:** Support for multiple deployment environments (Development, Staging, Production).
*   **Automated Health Checks:** Services to monitor the health and integrity of all system modules.
*   **Backup & Restore:** Robust tools for creating and restoring full system backups.

## Target Users

*   **Citizens:** For accessing public services and information.
*   **Ketua RT (Head of Neighborhood Association):** For initial verification and community management.
*   **Ketua RW (Head of Community Unit):** For second-level verification and area management.
*   **Staf Kelurahan (Village/District Staff):** For final approval, document issuance, and system administration.
*   **Pimpinan (Leadership):** For monitoring performance and viewing executive reports.
