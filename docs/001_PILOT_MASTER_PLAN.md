## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the master plan for the pilot implementation of the WK Community OS at Kelurahan Kebonjati. The purpose of this pilot is to deploy the system in a controlled, real-world environment to validate its functionality, usability, performance, and overall value before a full-scale rollout.

This plan serves as the central guiding document for all pilot-related activities and references other detailed planning documents.

## 2. Pilot Objectives

The primary objectives of the Kebonjati Pilot are:

*   **Validate Core Functionality:** To test the end-to-end workflows of key modules (Citizen, Letter, Complaint) with real users and data.
*   **Measure Performance:** To assess the system's performance and stability under normal operational load.
*   **Gather User Feedback:** To collect qualitative and quantitative feedback from all user roles (Citizen, RT, RW, Kelurahan Staff) to identify areas for improvement.
*   **Assess Business Value:** To measure the impact of the system against predefined success criteria, such as reduced processing times and improved data accuracy.
*   **Confirm Operational Readiness:** To ensure the system is ready for a full production launch, including training, support, and maintenance procedures.
