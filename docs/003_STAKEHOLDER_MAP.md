## 1. Overview

This document identifies the key stakeholders involved in the Kebonjati Pilot and maps their level of interest and influence on the project. Understanding our stakeholders is crucial for effective communication and management.

## 2. Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder | Role / Title | Interest (1-5) | Influence (1-5) | Key Expectations |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Lurah Kebonjati** | Head of Kelurahan | 5 | 5 | Improved public service efficiency, positive citizen feedback, successful digitization. |
| **Project Sponsor** | Executive Leadership | 5 | 5 | Pilot success, measurable ROI, smooth transition to production. |
| **Project Manager** | Implementation Team | 5 | 4 | On-time, on-budget delivery, meeting all success criteria. |
| **Kelurahan Staff** | End Users (Admin) | 4 | 3 | System is easy to use, reduces manual work, is reliable. |
| **Ketua RW** | End Users (Approver) | 4 | 3 | Simple approval process, clear dashboard, easy communication with RTs and citizens. |
| **Ketua RT** | End Users (Approver) | 4 | 3 | Simple verification process, accurate citizen data, easy to use on mobile. |
| **Citizens** | End Users (Public) | 3 | 2 | Easy access to services, transparent status tracking, faster response times. |
| **Technical Lead** | Implementation Team | 5 | 4 | Stable system, minimal critical bugs, successful deployment. |
| **Trainer** | Implementation Team | 4 | 2 | Users understand the system, training materials are effective. |

### Key

*   **Interest:** How much the stakeholder is affected by the project's outcome.
*   **Influence:** How much the stakeholder can impact the project's direction and success.
