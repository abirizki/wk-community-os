# Pilot Program - Success Criteria

The pilot program will be considered successful if the following measurable criteria are met by the end of the 4-week period.

---

## 1. User Adoption & Engagement

*   **Criterion:** At least 80% of identified pilot users log in to the system at least once per week.
*   **Measurement:** System access logs.

*   **Criterion:** At least 10 real (or simulated real) SKTM letter requests are processed end-to-end through the system.
*   **Measurement:** `LetterRepository` data.

*   **Criterion:** At least 5 real (or simulated real) complaints are processed end-to-end through the system.
*   **Measurement:** `ComplaintRepository` data.

## 2. System Performance & Stability

*   **Criterion:** No more than 1 "Critical" or "High" priority bug is discovered in a core workflow.
*   **Measurement:** `IssueLog.md`.

*   **Criterion:** The average page load time for dashboards remains under 3 seconds.
*   **Measurement:** Manual timing or frontend performance monitoring.

## 3. User Satisfaction

*   **Criterion:** The average score for "Overall Experience" on the `FeedbackForm.md` is 3.5 or higher (out of 5).
*   **Measurement:** Analysis of submitted feedback forms.

*   **Criterion:** At least 75% of official pilot users (RT, RW, Kelurahan) "Agree" or "Strongly Agree" (score of 4 or 5) with the statement "I believe this system is ready for a full-scale launch" on the `EvaluationForm.md`.
*   **Measurement:** Analysis of submitted evaluation forms.
