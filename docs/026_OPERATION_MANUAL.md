# Operation Manual

This manual provides a guide for the day-to-day operation of the WK Community OS for different user roles.

---

## 1. For Citizens

### 1.1. Requesting a Letter (e.g., SKTM)
1.  Log in to the citizen portal.
2.  Navigate to "Layanan Surat" from the main menu or dashboard.
3.  Select the desired letter type (e.g., "Surat Keterangan Tidak Mampu").
4.  Fill in any required fields (most data is pre-filled).
5.  Upload the required document attachments.
6.  Click "Ajukan Permohonan" to submit.
7.  You can track the status of your request in the "Riwayat Surat" section.

### 1.2. Submitting a Complaint
1.  Log in to the citizen portal.
2.  Navigate to "Layanan Aduan".
3.  Click "Ajukan Keluhan Baru".
4.  Select the appropriate category and sub-category.
5.  Provide a clear title, detailed description, and location of the issue.
6.  Upload a photo if applicable.
7.  Click "Ajukan Keluhan".
8.  You can track the status using the provided tracking number or in the "Riwayat Aduan" section.

## 2. For RT/RW Officials

### 2.1. Approving a Letter Request
1.  Log in to your dashboard.
2.  A new notification will appear for pending requests.
3.  Click the notification to view the request details.
4.  Review the citizen's data and attachments.
5.  Click "Setujui" to approve and forward to the next level, or "Tolak" to reject the request (a reason will be required).

### 2.2. Verifying a Complaint
1.  Log in to your dashboard.
2.  New complaints in your area will appear in your task list or as notifications.
3.  Review the complaint details.
4.  Click "Verifikasi" to confirm the complaint is valid and forward it to the Kelurahan for assignment.

## 3. For Kelurahan Staff

### 3.1. Finalizing a Letter
1.  Log in to the Kelurahan dashboard.
2.  In the "Surat Masuk" queue, find the letter request that has been approved by both RT and RW.
3.  Perform a final review.
4.  Click "Setujui & Terbitkan" to automatically generate the final PDF document.

### 3.2. Assigning a Complaint
1.  In the "Aduan Masuk" queue, find the complaint that has been verified by the RT/RW.
2.  Review the complaint and determine the appropriate department or officer.
3.  Click "Tugaskan" and select the assigned officer. The complaint will now be forwarded to that officer's task list.

### 3.3. Broadcasting a Notification
1.  Navigate to the "Notifikasi" or "Pengumuman" section.
2.  Click "Buat Broadcast Baru".
3.  Define the target audience (e.g., a specific RT, an entire RW, or all citizens).
4.  Write the title and body of the announcement.
5.  Click "Kirim" to send the broadcast.
