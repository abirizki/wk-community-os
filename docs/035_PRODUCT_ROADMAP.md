# Product Roadmap

This document outlines the planned future development for the WK Community OS platform.

---

## Q4 2026 - "Community Engagement"

*   **[Feature] Mobile Application:** Develop a dedicated mobile application (iOS/Android) for citizens to access services, receive push notifications, and interact with the community.
*   **[Feature] Social Assistance Module:** A dedicated module to manage the distribution of social assistance (Bansos), including eligibility checks and distribution tracking.
*   **[Feature] Posyandu Module:** A module for managing child health monitoring activities at the local Posyandu.
*   **[Improvement] Advanced Analytics:** Introduce predictive analytics for complaint hotspots and demographic trends.

## Q1 2027 - "Financial Integration"

*   **[Feature] PBB Payment Module:** Integration with local payment gateways to allow citizens to check and pay their PBB (Pajak Bumi dan Bangunan) tax online.
*   **[Feature] Local Business (UMKM) Directory:** A module for local businesses to register and promote their services to the community.
*   **[Improvement] Internationalization (i18n):** Add support for multiple languages in the user interface.

## Q2 2027 - "Inter-Agency Collaboration"

*   **[Feature] API Gateway:** Develop a secure, public-facing API gateway to allow for integration with other government systems (e.g., Dukcapil, regional disaster management).
*   **[Feature] Digital Signature Integration:** Integrate with a certified digital signature provider (e.g., BSrE) to create legally binding digital documents.
*   **[Improvement] Data Warehouse Integration:** Provide tools to export and sync data to a dedicated data warehouse (e.g., BigQuery) for more advanced business intelligence.

## Long-Term Vision

*   **AI-Powered Assistance:** An AI chatbot to assist citizens with common questions and guide them through service requests.
*   **Smart City Integration:** Integration with IoT devices for monitoring public infrastructure (e.g., smart street lights, flood sensors).
*   **Marketplace & Self-Service Deployment:** A platform for other Kelurahan or local governments to easily deploy and customize their own instance of WK Community OS.
