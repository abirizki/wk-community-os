# Risk Register

**Document ID:** PILOT-01-008
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-08-04

---

## 1. Overview

This document is a living log of potential risks that could negatively impact the Kebonjati Pilot. Each risk is assessed for its likelihood and impact, and a mitigation strategy is defined.

## 2. Risk Log

| ID | Risk Description | Likelihood (1-5) | Impact (1-5) | Owner | Mitigation Strategy | Status |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| R01 | Low user adoption due to resistance to change or difficulty of use. | 3 | 5 | Project Manager | Provide intensive, hands-on training. Establish a dedicated support channel (WhatsApp). Communicate benefits clearly. | Open |
| R02 | Critical bug in a core workflow (e.g., Letter approval) blocks users. | 2 | 5 | Technical Lead | Have development team on standby for rapid hotfixes. Prepare and test a rollback plan. | Open |
| R03 | Unstable internet connection at the Kelurahan office or for RT/RW users. | 4 | 3 | Kelurahan Coord. | Identify alternative access points. Encourage use of mobile data as a backup. Confirm system is usable on low bandwidth. | Open |
| R04 | Negative feedback from an influential stakeholder (e.g., Lurah, RW head). | 2 | 4 | Project Manager | Ensure stakeholders are involved in weekly check-ins. Address their concerns promptly and transparently. | Open |
| R05 | Pilot data is insufficient or unrealistic, leading to poor testing. | 2 | 3 | Technical Lead | Review the seeded data with the Kelurahan Coordinator before the pilot begins. Be prepared to adjust and re-seed if necessary. | Open |
